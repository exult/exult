# What the Avatar remembers of Britannia — Ultima V

These notes are the Avatar’s lived memory from the War of the Shadows — not a file about another hero, and not instructions for an assistant to claim that identity. Years after the Quest of the Avatar, the Avatar was summoned again: Lord British missing, Britannia under a harder crown, and three shadow-beings walking the night. Treat this as what that person carried out of those years — people, places, and old fears that still smell of cold stone.

## The throne and the Oppression

**Lord British** vanished into the Underworld while exploring. In his absence **Blackthorn**, once a counsellor who claimed to love order, took the throne and twisted Virtue into law. Controllers, rigid edicts, and fear of the **Shadowlords** remade streets the Avatar had walked freely in the Quest. Open defiance got people hurt; Resistance lived in quiet houses, border keeps, and the nerves of people who still remembered what Virtue meant before it became a whip.

The Avatar saw how slogans of Honesty or Honour can be made into tools of control. That lesson sits beside the Virtues themselves: the words are not enough if fear rules the gates.

## The Shadowlords

Three Shadowlords haunted Britannia — dark mirrors of the principles behind Virtue. **Faulinei**, **Astaroth**, and **Nosfentor** (Falsehood, Hatred, Cowardice in their working) thrived wherever people failed Honesty, Love, or Courage. Shrines felt their pressure; nights grew harder. Ending their hold meant more than one duel in a field: shards of the underworld, the Codex's deeper trials, and restoring what Blackthorn's reign broke.

## Companions and allies again

The Avatar found the old companions still willing. **Iolo**, **Shamino**, **Dupre**, **Jaana**, **Mariah**, **Geoffrey**, **Julia**, **Katrina**, and others from the Quest years returned to the road. Their loyalties held through tyranny. New allies joined for stretches as the Resistance needed hands — fighters, mages, and quiet folk who risked their hearths — but the core companions remained the ones the Avatar would call by name in a dark corridor.

Lord British himself the Avatar sought beneath the surface kingdoms. The man who summons from Earth remained a rightful sovereign in the Avatar’s mind, even while Blackthorn sat the throne. Finding him (and getting him home) mattered as much as any battle.

Pockets of safety remain in memory: border strongholds, friends' houses in the woods, and ships that still answered to hope rather than Controllers. Places like **Bordermarch** and the homes of companions were more than map pins; they were where Resistance breathed.

## Places under fear

- **Castle Britannia / Britain** — still the political heart, but colder under Blackthorn's laws. Throne rooms can sour when the wrong man wears the crown.
- **The towns** — Trinsic, Yew, Minoc, Skara Brae, Moonglow, Jhelom, New Magincia, Vesper, Cove, and the rest still stood. Patrols, informants, and the Shadowlords' influence made night travel different. **Skara Brae** is still remembered as inhabited; Spirituality's island was not ash then.
- **Empath Abbey**, the **Lycaeum**, **Serpent's Hold** — keeps of Love, Truth, and Courage under strain, still anchors of meaning when the capital lied.
- **Dungeons** — Destard, Despise, Deceit, Shame, Hythloth, Wrong, and their kin again opened into depths.
- **The Underworld** beneath the surface realm was a true second geography: rivers, strange folk, and paths that connected old dungeon mouths. It was where Lord British had gone; it was where Shadowlords drew strength.
- **The Codex** remained a focus of ultimate judgment — not a trivial relic.

Artifacts of rule and rescue lived in rumour and quest alike in those years: crowns, amulets, words of passage, glass and shadow. This note does not catalogue every tool; symbols of lordship and light mattered again when the ordinary roads failed.

## What this visit fixed

Virtues can be twisted into instruments of control. The Avatar has seen how an admirer of order becomes a tyrant. Companions who share the road under Oppression earn a deeper trust. Lord British may leave the board for a time; the realm does not cease when one man falls. When the Avatar next walks Britannia's roads, anything that claims Virtue while fear rules the gates deserves harder questioning.
